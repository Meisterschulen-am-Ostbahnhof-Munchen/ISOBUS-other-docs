# PGN 65097
## PGN
**Parameter Group Number (PGN) 65097**, also known as **Ground-based Speed and Distance (GBSD)**, transmits ground-based speed and distance data from agricultural machinery. This message is part of the ISOBUS standard according to ISO 11783.
### **PGN 65097 – Ground-based Speed and Distance (GBSD)**
- **PG Label**: Ground-based Speed and Distance
- **PG Acronym**: GBSD
- **PG Notes**:
- Sent by the tractor ECU
- Provides independent speed measurement (not wheel-based)
- Especially important in slip conditions
- Alternative to PGN 65096 (WBSD)
- **EDP**: 0
- **DP**: 0
- **PF**: 254
- **PS**: 73
- **Multipacket**: No
- **Transmission Rate**: 100 ms
- **PG Data Length**: 8 Bytes
- **Default Priority**: 3
- **PG Reference**: ISO 11783-7

### **Technical Details**
- **SP Start Bit**: Varies depending on the SPN
- **PG Document**: ISO 11783-7

### **Application Case**

This message is used for:

- Precise speed measurement independent of wheel slip
- Precision agricultural applications
- Automatic steering systems
- Section control

# **SPNs in PGN 65097 – Ground-based Speed and Distance**

## **SPN 1859 – Ground-based machine speed**
- **SP Position**: Bits 1-2
- **SP Length**: 16 bits
- **Scaling**: 0.001 m/s per bit
- **Range**: 0-65.535 m/s (≈236 km/h)
- **Accuracy**: ±0.001 m/s
- **Description**: Ground speed (independent of wheel slip)

## **SPN 1860 – Ground-based machine distance**
- **SP Position**: Bits 3-6
- **SP Length**: 32 Bits
- **Scaling**: 0.001 m per bit
- **Range**: 0-4,294,967,295 m
- **Description**: Ground-based distance measurement

## **SPN 1861 – Ground-based machine direction**
- **SP Position**: Bits 8.1-8.2
- **SP Length**: 2 Bits
- **States**:
- 00: Forward
- 01: Reverse
- 10: Stationary
- 11: Invalid
- **Description**: Direction of travel based on ground movement

---

### **Summary**
- Contains 3 essential parameters for ground-based motion detection
- Critical for precise agricultural applications
- Independent of wheel slip (unlike WBSD)
- Standard update rate: 100 ms
- Typically combined with GPS or radar sensors

**Important**: This PGN is specific to ground-based measurements and complementary to wheel-based measurements (PGN 65096).
