# PGN 65254
## PGN
**Parameter Group Number (PGN) 65254**, also known as **Time/Date (TD)**, transmits current time and date information in the ISOBUS network. This message is important for time synchronization, logging, and timestamping in agricultural machinery.
**
### **PGN 65254 – Time/Date (TD)**
- **PG Label**: Time/Date
- **PG Acronym**: TD
- **PG Notes**:
- Sent on request
- Contains UTC time and local time zone offsets
- Typically provided by the tractor ECU
- Year offset from 1985 (value 0 = 1985)
- **EDP**: 0
- **DP**: 0
- **PF**: 254
- **PS**: 230
- **Multipacket**: No
- **Transmission Rate**: On request
- **PG Data Length**: 8 Bytes
- **Default Priority**: 6
- **PG Reference**: ISO 11783-7

### **Technical Details**
- **PG Document**: ISO 11783-7

### **Use Case**

This message is used for:

- Time synchronization between ECUs
- Timestamps for process data logging
- Documentation of working hours
- Correct time information in the task controller and FMIS

# **SPNs in PGN 65254 – Time/Date (TD)**

## **SPN 959 – Seconds**
- **SP Position**: Byte 1
- **SP Length**: 8 bits
- **Scaling**: 0.25 s per bit
- **Offset**: 0 s
- **Range**: 0 to 59.75 s
- **Unit**: s
- **Description**: Seconds (0-59)

## **SPN 960 – Minutes**
- **SP Position**: Byte 2
- **SP Length**: 8 bits
- **Scaling**: 1 minute per bit
- **Offset**: 0 min
- **Range**: 0 to 59 min
- **Unit**: min
- **Description**: Minutes (0-59)

## **SPN 961 – Hours**
- **SP Position**: Byte 3
- **SP Length**: 8 Bits
- **Scaling**: 1 h per bit
- **Offset**: 0 h
- **Range**: 0 to 23 h
- **Unit**: h
- **Description**: Hours (0-23)

## **SPN 963 – Month**
- **SP Position**: Byte 4
- **SP Length**: 8 Bits
- **Scaling**: 1 month per bit
- **Offset**: 0 month
- **Range**: 1 to 12 months
- **Unit**: month
- **Description**: Month (1-12)

## **SPN 962 – Day**
- **SP Position**: Byte 5
- **SP Length**: 8 Bits
- **Scaling**: 0.25 days per bit
- **Offset**: 0 days
- **Range**: 0.25 to 31.75 days
- **Unit**: days
- **Description**: Day (0.25 steps, 1-31)

## **SPN 964 – Year**
- **SP Position**: Byte 6
- **SP Length**: 8 Bits
- **Scaling**: 1 year per bit
- **Offset**: 1985 year
- **Range**: 1985 to 2235
- **Unit**: year
- **Description**: Year (Offset 1985, Value 0 = 1985)

## **SPN 1601 – Local minute offset**
- **SP Position**: Byte 7
- **SP Length**: 8 bits
- **Scaling**: 1 minute per bit
- **Offset**: -125 minutes
- **Range**: -59 to 59 minutes
- **Unit**: minutes
- **Description**: Local time offset in minutes (for time zone)

## **SPN 1602 – Local hour offset**
- **SP Position**: Byte 8
- **SP Length**: 8 bits
- **Scaling**: 1 hour per bit
- **Offset**: -125 hours
- **Range**: -23 to 23 hours
- **Unit**: hours
- **Description**: Local time offset in hours (for time zone)

---

### **Summary**
- PGN 65254 contains 8 SPNs for complete time/date information.
- Time zone is specified by the local minute/hour offset.

`` - The year has an offset of 1985.

- Important for logging and synchronization in the ISOBUS network.
