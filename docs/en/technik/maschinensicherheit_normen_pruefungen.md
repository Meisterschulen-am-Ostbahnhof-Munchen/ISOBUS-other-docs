## 1. Statutory Inspections in the Agricultural and Construction Machinery Industry

### DGUV Regulations & Accident Prevention Regulations

- **DGUV Regulation 70/73 (Accident Prevention Regulations):** Regular inspection of vehicles and winches, lifting and pulling equipment by qualified personnel.

- **Inspection Reports:** Legally compliant recording, documentation of defects, and inspection sticker.

### Safety Inspection (SP) & Emissions Test (AU)

- **SP according to § 29 StVZO:** Recurring inspection of the chassis, suspension, steering, braking system, and wheels of high-speed tractors (> 40 km/h) and construction machinery.

- **Emissions & Exhaust Gas Test:** Verification of compliance with opacity limits or particle number (PN measurement from Stage V).

## 2. Machine Safety & CE Conformity

### Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC & Regulation (EU) 2023/1230
- **Risk assessment according to EN ISO 12100:** Identification of hazards, design safeguards, technical safeguards, user information.

- **Safety-related parts of control systems:** EN ISO 13849-1 (Performance Level PL a to PL e) and AgPL (Agricultural Performance Level).

### Type Approval EU 167/2013 (Tractor Mother Regulation)

- Harmonised design and testing regulations for agricultural and forestry vehicles in the EU (brakes, occupant protection, lighting).
