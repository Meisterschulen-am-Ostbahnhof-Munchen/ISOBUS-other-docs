## 1. Compressed Air Generation & Treatment

### Compressor & Pressure Regulation
- **Piston Compressors:** Single- and two-stage compression.

- **Pressure Regulators:** Controls the cut-in and cut-out pressure (e.g., 8.1 bar / 10.0 bar).

- **Air Drying Systems:** Single- and dual-chamber air dryers with cartridge and automatic regeneration phase.

### Multi-Circuit Protection Valves
- Protection of individual brake circuits (service brake circuits 1 & 2, parking brake, auxiliary consumers).

--

## 2. Trailer Brake Systems (1- & 2-Line Systems)

### 2-Line Compressed Air Brake System (Standard according to EU 167/2013)

- **Supply Line (Red):** Constant supply pressure from the tractor unit to the trailer (approx. 7.0–8.5 bar).

- **Control Line (Yellow):** Transmits the brake pressure-dependent control pressure when the foot brake is applied (0–7.5 bar).

- **Trailer Brake Valve:** Controls the pressure supply from the trailer reservoir to the brake cylinders.

### ALB (Automatic Load-Dependent Brake Force Control)
- Mechanical linkage on the trailer axle or pneumatic actuation with air suspension.

- Adjusts the brake pressure to the load condition (empty, partial, and full load) to prevent overbraking/wheel lockup.

--

## 3. ABS / EBS Systems in Agricultural and Construction Machinery

- **Electronic Braking System (EBS):** Senses wheel speeds (inductive/Hall sensors) and electronically modulates the brake pressures.

- **Circular Connector:** ISO 7638 connector for power supply and CAN data transmission of the EBS between the tractor unit and trailer.
