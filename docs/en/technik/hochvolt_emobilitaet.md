## 1. Safety Rules & DGUV Regulations

### Legal Basis & Qualification
- **DGUV Information 209-093 / DGUV 200-005:** Qualification for work on vehicles with high-voltage systems (HV levels 1, 2, and 3).

- **Voltage Limits:** AC voltage > 30 ext{V AC}$ or DC voltage > 60 ext{V DC}$ is considered high voltage.

### 5 Safety Rules for De-energizing

1. Disconnect (pull the HV service disconnector / main switch).

2. Secure against reconnection (padlock / warning sign).

3. Verify absence of voltage (two-pole test adapter / measurement at test points $+$, $-$, PE).


4. Grounding and short-circuiting (from 1,000 ext{ V}$).

5. Cover or barricade adjacent live parts.

## 2. Battery Technology & Power Electronics

### Lithium-Ion Batteries & BMS

- **Cell Chemistry:** NMC (Nickel-Manganese-Cobalt), LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate).

- **BMS (Battery Management System):** Cell balancing, monitoring of cell voltage, temperature, and insulation resistance (ISO monitor).

### Inverters & Electric Motors

- **Inverters:** Conversion of DC battery voltage to 3-phase AC for the drive system.

- **Motor Types:** Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors (PSM), Induction Motors (ASM) with regenerative braking capability.
